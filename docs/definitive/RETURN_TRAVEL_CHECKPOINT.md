# Return travel and transition recovery checkpoint

The earned campaign can return between previously reached Mars and Fog Moon orbits from a safe owned Warship or bridge. First visits keep their authored flight missions. Return travel preserves actual hull/shields, fighter, credits, recruits, quests and all three upgrade tracks; it grants no patrol or chapter rewards. Surface and unfinished-flight map choices explicitly continue the current mission. Legacy discoveries do not launch unimplemented definitive chapters. A completed Fog voyage cannot restart with already claimed wave receipts.

The bridge scene is constructed before its entry checkpoint commits. Loading, construction, storage, stale revision and cancellation failures dispose candidates and preserve the previous scene/save. The owned ship enters at the bridge; earlier boarding resumes its real room. Retry cards are centered above the prior HUD with 44px minimum buttons. Map actions and cleared status use #00FF00; active threats use red. Rebuilt quest receipts drive Mars/Fog targets instead of inherited legacy boss names.
