# CODED — Story and Quest Script v1

Companion to `CODED-DEFINITIVE-20260906-v1`. Newly authored fictional game material, not recovered biography or a claim about real financial institutions. Astra may refine pacing, barks and secondary names while preserving the mission purpose and character identities. These are implementation-ready writing inputs; adapt them to the actual dialogue schema rather than treating Markdown as executable game data.

## Story rule

People first; terminology second. Show the consequence of stolen liquidity through dark buildings, stalled transport, trapped crews and dry growing beds. The Suits call their controls protection; the game shows the discrepancy. Victory means restoring service and returning choice, not enriching the hero. The capital ship is captured because it contains the route ledger needed to follow the theft.

The recurring question is: **Who closed the route, and where did the flow go?** Each destination supplies a new answer. The final twist is not that clarity is bad: FINAL CLARITY is the Directorate's misleading name for a permanent freeze.

## Earth chapter — complete scene sequence

Mandatory conversations pause danger, retain skip/log controls and apply quest effects once. Short optional comms never take flight control. New scene IDs below are proposals and must be registered before use.

**Objective:** DEFEND EARTH // BREAK THE BLOCKADE. The fighter launches into orbital combat. Nothing requires a long cutscene before first movement.

## Boarding quest graph

`hangar_safe → security_relay → rescue_junction → engineering_power → command_access → core_defeated → bridge_secured → departure_ready`.

The optional cache branches from rescue_junction and reconnects before command_access. It is not mandatory for beating the Core. Doors are driven by explicit flags with a visible reason when locked. Rooms become safe when their finite encounter ends. Cleared rooms do not refill indefinitely. Terminal effects and reward IDs are idempotent. An interrupted conversation must not strand the quest between “cost paid” and “door opened.”

Shop introduction follows bridge security, when there is time to read. Mr Zamn offers defensive hardware at a terminal; Stone's remote console explains navigation. One repair and one clearly differentiated upgrade is enough for the first shop. More items follow progression, not a page of unavailable buttons.

## Later worlds — authored arc and character payoffs

**Mars:** Open the relief pumps and break the Margin Warden's extraction circuit. Corn is now encountered at a relief site, giving a physical payoff to the Earth comms introduction. “Good news: the soil still works. Bad news: the machinery thinks it owns the rain.” Reward a practical repair/traversal capability; reveal freight routes heading into Fog Moon.

**Fog Moon:** Learn to distinguish a true signal from decoys. Boo is encountered following the residue of stolen transfers, not summoned as an unexplained party member. “They hid the route. They forgot the trail.” Use limbless ghost animation and a limited reveal ability. The Fog Relay Citadel's defeat uncovers the industrial destination.

**Bullion Reach:** LEX coordinates a convoy that cannot leave while a Siege Engine locks the route. “A reserve nobody can use isn't protection. It's a hostage.” Restore passage and gain logistics/shops. Reveal two ways to the next evidence node: the unstable Rugfall route or fortified SEC Outpost.

**Rugfall:** Baited shortcuts and collapsing paths express the rug-pull theme. V4X provides evidence only after the player secures a terminal route; never reveal the concealed face. “Don't trust the banner. Follow the record.” An optional counter-trap unlock supports alternate routes, not mandatory progression the other branch cannot obtain.

**SEC Outpost:** Niall is trying to get a corroborated transmission past censorship. “A signal isn't free if someone else owns the off switch.” Destroy/disable the Enforcement Tower and unlock a broadcast route. He remains a signal specialist, not a heavy gunner.

**Whale Haven:** Large-scale docks and fleet geometry contrast with earlier fighter lanes. Optimystic Prime's mobile command assists against heavy signatures: “A big ship isn't a plan. A connected fleet is.” Secure fleet passage and open the Abyssal Vault without turning its population into collateral damage.

**Liquidity Depths:** The player sees the drain itself: streams redirected into one distant origin. Use linked power controls, siphon timing and leech pressure. Completing the Drain Core reroutes flow locally and exposes the command destinations. Ally support is based only on characters actually recruited.

**Court Nexus:** The Judgment Engine authenticates fabricated orders. A readable evidence/terminal interaction changes the battle's defenses. This is a fictional machine-world, not a simulation of real court procedure. Its completion reveals one verified access route to Zero Point.

**Regulatory Crown:** The Crown Fortress coordinates surviving batteries. Coordinated combat and a command-relay objective expose the alternative access route. Neither branch requires a specific optional ally; the ship supplies baseline tools, while recruited allies make different approaches possible.

**Clarity Zero:** FINAL CLARITY guards an order that would permanently freeze the network. Defeat its guardians, expose the Zero Point and interrupt the final command. The central choice is resolved by the hero's established motive: do not seize a universal master key for personal rule; restore distributed control. Recruited characters contribute optional lines and support, with functional generic ship-system fallback for missed side branches.

Final exchange:

Core authority: “One command. One network. No uncertainty.”

XRPMan: “No choice, either.”

Stone: “We can redirect everything through this bridge.”

XRPMan: “No. Send it home.”

**Epilogue:** Show restored locations, public transmissions, allies on the persistent ship and revisitable side routes. The map distinguishes cleared, optional and unfinished content. Never call a locked empty node a completed planet.

## Dialogue acceptance

Every scene has an actual trigger, registered speaker, text, input behavior and quest/reward effect or an explicit “no effect.” Capture readability on a phone. Avoid unexplained teleports: a comms portrait does not mean the character is physically aboard. Use short conversations; put optional worldbuilding into logs/NPC follow-ups. No invented voice actor, voice clone, ElevenLabs key or paid service is required for this version.
