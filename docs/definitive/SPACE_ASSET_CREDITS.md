# Space surface credits

Earth surface: Reto Stöckli, NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation, January 2004. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/ . The runtime derivative is resampled to 1024×512 and embedded in an original sphere mesh.

Mars surface: NASA/JPL/Caltech; Viking spacecraft imagery processed by the USGS. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars/ . The runtime derivative is resampled to 1024×512 and embedded in an original sphere mesh.

These are factual image-source credits, with no NASA affiliation or endorsement. NASA media guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ . No agency logos, people or branding are used.

Five enemy ships are original Blender geometry studies based on the established CODED silhouettes. Fast Scout retains the narrow multi-prong identity. Whale Scout is an original broad, blunt four-pod missile ship, correcting the duplicated original source identity. Armor uses the same original generated surface tile and authored material maps as the Warship. Editable masters and reference imagery remain private. Models and materials are provisional production assets; final concept/finish review remains.
