# Draft campaign playtest

Preview: https://coded-xrp-hunt-for-clarity-git-astra-de-b704e5-xrpmans-projects.vercel.app

The rebuilt connected route reaches Mars restoration: Earth flight → actual fighter landing → 3D boarding/Mr Zamn/Core → captured ship flight → Mars relief/Corn → excavation/Margin Warden → Dash and return. Later destinations remain unfinished in this definitive build. This is a development preview with a separate save; no release or merge.

Section links create isolated test saves with the prerequisites needed to inspect that section. They do not represent earned campaign progression:

Tap RESUME. On foot, drag the open game area to move and hold BLAST with another finger. SHIELD toggles defense; REPAIR restores damaged vitals. INTERACT talks or operates the nearby control. After the Warden, DASH works while moving/firing. On a keyboard use WASD/arrows, Space, E, Q, R, Shift and P. Flight retains drag steering and held GUNS.

At the excavation, clear four drones and operate the gate pedestal. Disable both red towers within twelve seconds, then fire at the exposed central control. Leave red lanes/circles before they activate. After victory, try Dash and follow the service road south to return to Corn. Reload should preserve completion.

Try portrait and landscape, cancellation/rotation while holding controls, shield/repair timing, dialogue, death/retry and Full/Low detail. The builder's native touch checks are desktop emulation. Actual phone feel, thermal behavior and sustained performance remain owner/device review items. Materials and geometry still need further refinement toward the requested realism.
