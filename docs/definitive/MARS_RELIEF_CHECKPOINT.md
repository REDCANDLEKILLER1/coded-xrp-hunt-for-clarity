# Mars relief-site opening

This checkpoint extends the earned Earth-to-Mars journey onto an actual surface site. It is the opening foothold on Mars, not completion of Mars's Margin Warden campaign or the later worlds.

The captured Warship must physically reach within 480 m of the existing Mars approach beacon. At approach speed, DESCEND transfers to the saved original fighter. Its real mesh descends before XRPMan becomes controllable. Surface reloads restore the earned site checkpoint. Loading and save failures retain the previous scene; returning to orbit uses the existing capital flight and its retained pose.

Corn is a physical rigged ally at an open relief canopy. His meeting explains three seized water plants. Eight finite defenders hold the valves; clearing enemies allows a nearby control to be released without destroying friendly machinery. Each successful save turns its plant and conduit green and grants 40 fictional salvage once. All three restored plants activate the crop beds. Returning to Corn recruits him and grants a separate hero field-repair ability: up to 40 vitals, with a 20-second recharge. The inherited Ledger Shield also has a real damage/charge consumer. Fighter and capital upgrades are preserved separately.

Touch movement and BLAST own separate pointers. Cancelling either pointer, pausing, entering dialogue, changing orientation and leaving the scene clear the appropriate held input. Mobile HUD checks caught a landscape overlap and a weak valve-range cue; both were corrected. DETAIL FULL/LOW changes rendered pixel density and dynamic shadows while retaining models, faction colors and mechanics.
