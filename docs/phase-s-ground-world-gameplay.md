# Phase S — Ground world gameplay

Phase S puts the original ground bank into live play without changing campaign length or boss balance.

## World layer

- Stage-specific mega towers, data spires, energy barriers, regulatory outposts, XRP billboards, and defense platforms scroll along the flight corridor.
- Props remain decorative and edge-anchored so they do not create misleading invisible collision.

## Interactive ground threats

- Six defense classes unlock progressively: basic, cannon, tower, laser, missile, and plasma.
- Each class has distinct durability, cadence, projectile speed, score, and selection weight.
- Clarity asteroids enter the flight lane as destructible collision hazards and do not fire.
- Existing weapon, collision, bomb-clear, scoring, and boss-gate cleanup paths apply to every variant.
- Sprite health bars now remain visible when original artwork loads.
